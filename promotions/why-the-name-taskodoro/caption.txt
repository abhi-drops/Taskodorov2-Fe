Why the name Taskodoro?

My first idea was to make a task + pomodoro app, so I named it Taskodoro. But on the second draft a complete rewrite and redesign happened.

The focus shifted to a simpler design: you get a simple app, and figure out more from the Apps and Settings tabs. So Pomodoro went from the main feature to a side feature in the Apps section. Then I forgot to build it till now… 😅

What do you think? Do we really need a Pomodoro feature for the sake of the name Taskodoro… 🙂 Or do I have to change the app name? 😂
